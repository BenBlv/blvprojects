    Bill of materials¶

    Note

    This list is in addition to the AM8 BOM.

    General¶

    • 7 × MGN12H carriage (4 for the Y axis, 1 for the X axis, 2 for the Z axis)
    • 3 × 2040 extrusion 313 mm (top, front, back)
    • 4 × 2040 extrusion 364 mm (Y axis)
    • 1 × 2020 extrusion 420 mm (X axis)
    • 2 × 608ZZ bearing, optional, only with the top threaded rod holders
    • 16 × HBLFSNF5 corner bracket, or the cheap ones I used
    • 2 × GT2 16T idler (3 mm bore)
    • 1 × 5 m GT2 fiberglass-reinforced belt, 6 mm wide, 2 mm pitch (the best belt I used)
    • 6 × rubber feet
    • 1 × PC4-M6 OD4 mm fitting, optional, for Greg's geared extruder
    Screws and nuts (16 items)
    Qty Part
    150 M5×10 button head socket cap screw
    8 M5×10 socket head cap screw
    6 M5×16 socket head cap screw
    160 M5 T-nut or M5×0.8 square nut
    1 M4×30 hex head (Y tensioner knob)
    20 M4×10 screw
    20 M4 nut
    20 M4 washer
    60 M3×8 socket head cap screw
    4 M3×20 socket head cap screw
    6 M3×20 screw
    1 M3×32 screw
    40 M3 T-nut
    4 M3 nut
    13 M3 washer
    1 M3 Nyloc

    Rails¶

    Two options for the MGN12H rails:

    1. Lower cost rails
    2. Genuine HIWIN rail set, more expensive but the best quality available

    Metal parts for extra rigidity:

    • Metal bed carriage for MGN carriages
    • Bottom metal plates, stronger replacements for the AM8 printed parts
    • Metal belt tensioner for the Y axis

    Choose your size¶

    • 2 × 2040 extrusion 440 mm (Z axis)
    • 3 × MGN12 350 mm rail (X and Z axis)
    • 2 × MGN12 400 mm rail (Y axis)
    • 4 × MGN12 400 mm rail
    • 1 × MGN12 360 or 350 mm rail
    • 2 × 2040 extrusion 500 mm (Z axis)
    • 2 × threaded rods 400 mm

    Hotend¶

    1. Trianglelab V6 hotend (1.75, 24V, PTFE), used in an earlier build
    2. Mellow hotend set (24V, PTFE), my choice
    3. Blurolls good quality V6 hotend
    4. Cheap V6 hotend clone

    Electronics¶

    • Duet WiFi clone v1.03 + 4.3" LCD + controller
    • All-metal E3D V6 hotend, genuine E3D from Filastruder.com, highly recommended
    • BMG dual-drive extruder / good metal BMG dual extruder / BMG clone extruder
    • 1 m Capricorn clone PTFE tube, 1.9 or 2 mm ID
    • Silicone sock to keep the nozzle temperature steady
    • Quick wire connectors
    • Spring steel sheet 220 × 220 + PEI sheet + magnetic sheet

    Optional¶

    • 1 m corrugated tube
    • Braided cable sleeve, Prusa style
    • DC step-down module LM2596, for 12V fans or an LED strip
    • 5 m LED strip 5050, 60 LEDs/m, 44-key, waterproof
    • Wago compact wiring connector
    • 500 pcs 3/32" G10 hardened steel balls for MGN12H rails (replacement)
    • 100K NTC 3950 thermistors (replacement if broken)
    • Brass brush for cleaning the hotend
    • 440-piece set of M3 DIN912 socket head screws

    Tools¶

    • Cable cutter
    • Tapping kit M3, M4, M5, M6, mainly for the corner holes
    • PTFE tube cutter
    • Copper wire terminal set
    • Desiccant for your dry box, the best I tested
    • Stainless steel digital caliper
    • Cable organizer
    • TS100 soldering iron, my favorite
    • Belt tension gauge, a 3D printed tool
