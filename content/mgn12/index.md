# BLV mgn12 mod

<div class="hero" markdown>

## Why

Linear bearings and Delrin wheel gantries are common in low and mid range printers as a cheap replacement for MGN rails - but at the expense of accuracy and reliability. Linear guided (MGN) rails are much more rigid, extremely precise and smooth. They let you print faster without losing quality, and because the rails are fixed to the frame, they prevent unwanted motion from bent threaded rods or the other axes. That's why high-end printers use them. So when I found cheap Chinese MGN rails on AliExpress, I thought: why not?

## Important

!!! warning "Patience required"
    Fixing the MGN rails can be very frustrating: they must be perfectly aligned and straight, or you'll get headaches. Don't start this project without tons of patience.

- **Design flexibility and source file** - I'm sharing the source file so the design can be improved; the STEP file is in the project ZIP. Please follow the license (Creative Commons - Attribution - Non-Commercial - Share-Alike).
- If you use the STEP file, don't forget to mark your work as a remix :)
- I also made a [build plate for Simplify3D](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2598588) for this project.

!!! note
    You may need to re-center the bed coordinates in the Marlin configuration, or move the Z extrusion to center the hotbed.

Seeing so many great community members upload photos and remixes is worth all the long hours I put into this project :) I'd really appreciate an "**I made one**" photo :)

## Mod variations - choose your style

1. **Anet A8 converted to [AM8](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2263216) (original height 440 mm) + BLV mgn12 mod** - if you already own an AM8 and don't want to change its height, get 350 mm rails instead of 400 mm.
2. **Anet A8 converted to [AM8](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2263216) + taller Z axis (500 mm) + BLV mgn12 mod** - build the AM8 with small changes:
    - instead of 2 × 440 mm 2040 extrusions → 2 × 500 mm (or longer) 2040 extrusions
    - instead of 2 × 340 mm 2040 extrusions → 4 × 364 mm 2040 extrusions
    - [longer 400 mm threaded rods](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/bcAVj7LS)

    You don't need to print all the AM8 parts - see [what to print](build.md#what-to-print).
3. **Anet [AM8](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2263216) + freestyle + BLV mgn12 mod** - the design is flexible, with separate parts you can fit as you wish. For a custom size with a bigger bed or a taller Z axis, choose your extrusion sizes and attach the modular parts to them.
