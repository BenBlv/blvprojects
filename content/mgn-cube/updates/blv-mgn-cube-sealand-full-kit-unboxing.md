# Sealand full kit unboxing

I decided to build another BLV mgn Cube, and this time it's going to be much easier, since I'm assembling it from the [full Sealand kit](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/cmcYPkdE).

!yt tGnT8uNTkVc

I'm a bit embarrassed to say that Peter from Sealand gave me a small symbolic discount on the shipping cost, although I didn't ask for it. Thank you, Blurolls Lu.

The kit arrived after about two weeks. I must say the packaging is absolutely fantastic. It has EPE foam cut to fit the specific parts, which is impressive. All the parts were there and nothing was missing, and it was nice to find a parts list in the package too.

The package had three parts:

1. Black panels, heated bed, spring steel, PEI
2. Rails, brackets and electronics
3. Frame and screws

The first thing I noticed was the anodized aluminum extrusions. It's great to have the profiles colored after cutting and not before (an expensive process). He also added four color samples to show me the colors, and I must say they are beautiful.

As expected from original HIWIN rails: perfection. To be honest, the rails are 25% of the kit's value and worth every penny.

I'm so happy right now and just wanted to share it with you. I hope you like the video I made ☺️

Two small things. First, I wish the Duet WiFi were v1.04 and not v1.03, although there is no difference in the quality or functionality of the board. Second, I don't really like the matte finish of the V6 and prefer it natural, but that's only a matter of personal taste.
