# Duet WiFi giveaway (finished)

Read the [original Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/groups/BenLevi/permalink/521983261862548/).

![](../assets/img/posts/075c2efeef31.jpg)

### Want a chance to win?

I wanted to thank you for your support and for just being here, sharing and helping others. I thought it would be great to do a giveaway of a Duet WiFi board, and it's going to be very simple:

1. Upload a **YouTube** video of your **BLV mgn Cube** 3D printer (yes, even if you haven't finished building it yet ☺️). Make sure your video's title includes the words "**BLV mgn Cube 3d printer**", and don't forget to add the project link to the video description: [**www.thingiverse.com/thing:3382718**](http://www.thingiverse.com/thing:3382718?fbclid=IwAR38PAcvSrRvvPFFH4bYwAj_O6fwJNzccDu-wrpBB6IMBUyG5ayd40h19-A). *Note: please upload the video to YouTube and not directly to Facebook.*
2. Add a comment on this post with your **YouTube** video link, and that's it. When the giveaway ends, I will run a script that randomly selects the lucky winner, who will get a brand new Duet WiFi board.

### Giveaway winners
