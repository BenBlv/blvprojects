WIRING

At this point, we will install all the necessary wiring.

The wiring process is a critical step and you must perform a double check on each wire you install.

The whole process itself can take around 3 hours and sometimes more, depending on you. If you are lucky and have the FYSETC kit the whole wiring process will take about an hour. FYSETC kit has a wiring diagram has an optimized wiring diagram, all the needed cables ready to be installed, all the stepper motors wires arranged in the right order, labeled and have plugs. They created a unique PCB for the neopixel rings (5 minutes installation) based on the original neopixek project - plug and play. so it saves a lot of time. so pay attention to the type of wiring diagram you are using.

Few tips

  • Please wire your 3d printer while the AC plug is not connected to any power source.

  • make sure to crimp every cable you are connecting to the PSU (FYSETC kit cables are already pre-crimped).

  • Don't mix negative and positive wires

  • double check every wire.
