# BOM - bill of materials

!!! note
    This list is in addition to the [AM8 BOM](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:2263216).

## General

- 7 × [MGN12H carriage](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/b4ZT5JAu) (4 for the Y axis, 1 for the X axis, 2 for the Z axis)
- 3 × 2040 extrusion 313 mm (top, front, back)
- 4 × 2040 extrusion 364 mm (Y axis)
- 1 × 2020 extrusion 420 mm (X axis)
- 2 × [608ZZ bearing](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/szFMbV04), optional, only with the top threaded rod holders
- 16 × HBLFSNF5 corner bracket, or [the cheap ones I used](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/pzdLzTJ6)
- 2 × [GT2 16T idler (3 mm bore)](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/ccTBT0U0)
- 1 × 5 m GT2 fiberglass-reinforced belt, 6 mm wide, 2 mm pitch ([the best belt I used](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/ciKvwgog))
- 6 × [rubber feet](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/c27ykKbA)
- 1 × [PC4-M6 OD4 mm fitting](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/hwPdjgM), optional, for Greg's geared extruder

## Rails

Two options for the MGN12H rails:

1. [Lower cost rails](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/b4ZT5JAu)
2. [Genuine HIWIN rail set](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/biPQl1lq), more expensive but the best quality available

**Metal parts** for extra rigidity:

- [Metal bed carriage for MGN carriages](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/HNqW428)
- [Bottom metal plates](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/tBlnoyY), stronger replacements for the AM8 printed parts
- [Metal belt tensioner for the Y axis](https://www.aliexpress.com/item/32917635708.html)

### Choose your size

=== "Option 1: original AM8 size"

    - 2 × 2040 extrusion 440 mm (Z axis)
    - 3 × [MGN12 350 mm rail](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/b4ZT5JAu) (X and Z axis)
    - 2 × [MGN12 400 mm rail](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/b4ZT5JAu) (Y axis)

=== "Option 2: taller Z"

    - 4 × [MGN12 400 mm rail](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/b4ZT5JAu)
    - 1 × [MGN12 360 or 350 mm rail](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/b4ZT5JAu)
    - 2 × 2040 extrusion 500 mm (Z axis)
    - 2 × [threaded rods 400 mm](http://s.click.aliexpress.com/e/bcAVj7LS)
