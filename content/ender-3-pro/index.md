## Why build it

**Fast, reliable, accurate**
:   Linear bearings and Delrin wheel gantries are common in low and mid range printers because they are cheap, but they cost accuracy and reliability. Linear guided (MGN) rails are much more rigid, extremely precise and smoother - shorter print times without losing quality. That's why high-end printers use them. I found cheap Chinese MGN rails on AliExpress and thought: why not? If you're tired of worn POM wheels and constantly tightening them, you came to the right place.

**Rigid parts**
:   This time I chose a different path. Printed parts are a bit flexible, and I wanted to take that out of the equation. CNC machined parts take the printer to the next level of accuracy. To test it I used a BT dial indicator logging into a database on my PC: after more than 2000 measuring loops of uniformity and deviation, the aluminum parts clearly improved the results compared to printed parts.

**Accessible**
:   CNC machined parts are expensive, right? To make the kit cheaper, I give all my work free for commercial use under one condition - my permission. Part of the cost of such parts is the design time (more than 200 hours here); saving that lets manufacturers sell the kit cheaper. For example, while 4 aluminum parts sell for $30+ on AliExpress, this kit has 16 pieces for $59, including belts, screws and shipping. Other aluminum + rails kits cost $300 and more. Now, I bet you're asking yourself something...

**Open source**
:   Where's the catch - why is this guy giving his work for free? A couple of years ago I got all my 3D knowledge from this awesome community. I really appreciate it and love this hobby, so this is my way of giving back - the same reason I made my earlier projects, the BLV mgn Cube and the BLV Anet mod. It's not easy, with a precious wife, 2 lovely little kids and a day job that has nothing to do with 3D printing. But this "thing" is my therapy (I think ☺️). 10 years ago the FDM patent expired, and sharing experience is what built everything we have now. Imagine if we had none of it and everyone kept their findings to themselves... I hope this gives you an idea of what I believe in, my friends :)

## Where to start

1. **Read, understand and prepare yourself** - read the project description, watch the 3D animation videos and make sure you understand the printer structure.
2. **Get the parts** - all the parts in the [BOM](bom.md), spare T-nuts and screws, and the tools you need. Also, mentally prepare yourself :)
3. **Build** - now the fun begins. A precise structure is crucial for excellent prints; several YouTube videos show how to align the Ender 3 Pro frame. Use the provided jigs when installing the MGN rails - it makes the assembly much easier. Take your time fixing the rails to the extrusions: this [quick video by 3D Distributed](http://youtube.com/watch?v=iX0U1ADH0MY) shows an easy way to align parallel rails.
4. **Electronics** - if you install the SKR Mini E3 V1.2 board, there's an excellent [YouTube installation guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VxCBDyvFSgA). It's the same with the Duet WiFi 1.04 board (if you're crazy enough).

    !yt VxCBDyvFSgA | SKR Mini E3 installation guide

5. **Software, tests and calibrations** - the scary part: you must know what you're doing, or follow the thousand YouTube videos on calibrating the Ender 3 Pro. My advice: replace the original firmware with a fresh Marlin, following one of the YouTube masters. Don't skip:
    - [ ] Wiring check + endstop check
    - [ ] Firmware update (a freshly updated Marlin)
    - [ ] Extruder calibration
    - [ ] Feed rate calibration
    - [ ] Hotend PID
    - [ ] Bed PID
    - [ ] Probe Z offset / Z offset
6. **Print** - enjoy your printer and give it love when needed :) Grease the rails with white lithium grease or light sewing machine oil, keep the rails clean and the belts tight. Clean the PEI build plate with alcohol after every print, and scratch new sheets a bit with brass wool.
7. **Post an "I made one"** - show the world your new printer and be proud of your creation :) I'd really appreciate pictures of your build; knowing it helped you makes all the hours worth it.
8. **Remix and share** - I'm 100% sure there are many ways to make this printer better, which is why the source file is included. Make it better and share your remixes with this awesome community :)
9. **Want to make it?** For commercial use, read the [legal license](license.md#legal-license).
